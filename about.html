Our Story

A family-rooted passion for authentic North Indian flavors, served with love since 2012.

Who We Are

Born From a Passion
for Authentic Flavors

The Royal Tandoor was founded in 2012 by the Kumar family — a household where cooking was never just about food. It was about tradition, memories, and the joy of sharing a wholesome meal together. What began as a modest 12-table restaurant in the heart of Kashipur's Main Market has blossomed into one of Uttarakhand's most celebrated dining destinations.

Every dish on our menu carries the soul of North Indian home cooking. We use no artificial flavors, no shortcuts — just fresh ingredients, traditional techniques, and the warmth of family recipes perfected over decades.

Today, we serve hundreds of guests every week — families celebrating milestones, friends catching up, and travelers discovering the authentic taste of Uttarakhand. And with every plate, we pour the same love and dedication that sparked our very first tandoor in 2012.

Our Journey

Milestones That
Shaped Us

From a small tandoor to a Kashipur institution — here's how we grew.

2012

Our Journey Begins

The Royal Tandoor opened its doors in Main Market, Kashipur with just 12 tables and a dream. Chef Ramesh Kumar's mother's recipes became our menu — and guests fell in love from day one.

2016

Growing With Our Customers

Growing demand led us to expand — adding 20 more seats, a separate family dining section, and a full Chinese menu to cater to diverse tastes in Kashipur.

2020

New Dining Experience

We renovated our interiors to create a warm, premium dining ambiance. Launched WhatsApp ordering so guests could enjoy Royal Tandoor flavors from home — a lifeline during challenging times.

2026

Serving With The Same Passion

Fourteen years on, the same clay tandoor, the same family recipes, the same heart. We continue to grow — but we never change what makes us us. The passion that lit our first tandoor still burns bright.

What Drives Us

Our Philosophy

Three pillars that guide every dish we cook and every guest we serve.

Authentic Flavors

We follow original North Indian recipes without compromise. No ready-made masalas, no shortcuts. Our spices are freshly ground, our gravies slow-cooked to perfection.

Fresh Every Day

Our kitchen starts fresh every morning. Vegetables sourced from local markets, dairy delivered daily, meats procured from trusted suppliers — nothing pre-cooked, nothing frozen.

Family First

We built The Royal Tandoor as a family restaurant. Every corner, every dish, and every interaction is designed to make you feel at home — because to us, our guests are family.

The Mastermind

Meet Chef
Ramesh Kumar

With over 18 years of experience in North Indian and Punjabi cuisine, Chef Ramesh Kumar is the heart and soul of The Royal Tandoor. Trained under seasoned master chefs in Delhi and Amritsar, Ramesh brings an extraordinary depth of knowledge to every recipe.

His philosophy is simple — great food requires patience, quality ingredients, and respect for tradition. Whether he's working the tandoor, blending spice mixes, or mentoring junior chefs, Ramesh brings the same meticulous passion to everything he creates.
