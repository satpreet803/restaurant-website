Our Story

A Taste You'll
Remember

Born from a passion for authentic North Indian cooking, The Royal Tandoor has been serving the people of Kashipur since 2012. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into one of the most loved dining destinations in Uttarakhand.

We source fresh ingredients daily, use traditional clay tandoor ovens, and follow recipes passed down through generations. Every meal is prepared with genuine care and a deep respect for culinary heritage.
